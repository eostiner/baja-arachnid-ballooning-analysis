│   │   └── optional sensitivity tables
│   ├── 07_final_trait_merge/

│   └── 08_grid25km_incidence/                         # generated by Step 10

│   ├── 02_incidence_matrices_25km/                    # generated/frozen copies
│   ├── 03_trait_tables/

## Minimum occurrence-table fields

- genus: `analysis_genus`, `genus`, or `trait_review_genus`
- latitude: `decimalLatitude`, `latitude`, or `decimal_latitude`
- longitude: `decimalLongitude`, `longitude`, or `decimal_longitude`
- stable occurrence identifier where available

## Trait table requirement

Every incidence-matrix genus must occur exactly once. Step 10T accepts the reviewed CSV or the authoritative Excel workbook `USE_GOOD_BalloonID_Baja_Arachnid_GenusSpecies_Long_D1_D4_AUTHORITATIVE.xlsx`; for that workbook it prefers the `Genus_Trait_Master_267` sheet. The authoritative reviewed export may use two complementary columns:

- `exclusive_tier`: D1, D2, D3, or D4 for genera with ballooning evidence; fixed N0 rows may be blank.
- `primary_C3_group`: Ballooning/C3, Non-ballooning/N0, or Excluded/D4.

Step 10T validates the two fields against one another and writes `07_reviewed_genus_trait_lookup_normalized.csv` with explicit `evidence_class` and `analysis_class` values. The primary analysis maps D1–D3/C3 to C3, retains fixed N0 as N0, and excludes D4. A legacy binary column alone is not accepted.

## Environmental rasters

Step 12B expects the retained ERA5-Land, MODIS EVI/phenology/land-cover, topographic, and JRC surface-water exports. Large rasters are not included in GitHub. Deposit frozen publication inputs in a DOI-backed data repository and document their checksums.
