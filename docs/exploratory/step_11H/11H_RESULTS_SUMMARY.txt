STEP 11H — SEPARATE C3 AND N0 GEOGRAPHIC GROUPING
==================================================

PURPOSE
Analyze ballooning-capable (C3) and fixed non-ballooning (N0) assemblages
separately to test whether their geographic composition groups differently.

DATA SUPPORT
C3: 87 genera, 148 occupied cells, 658 genus-cell presences.
N0: 140 genera, 176 occupied cells, 867 genus-cell presences.

STANDARDIZATION
Latitude bands: 22 occupied 25-km cells per band.
Ecoregions: 8 primary-eligible cells per ecoregion.
Monte Carlo iterations: 5000.

LATITUDE-BAND GROUPING
C3 nearest pair: 23-24N + 24-26N (56.4% of resamples).
N0 nearest pair: 24-26N + 26-28N (47.1% of resamples).
C3 most-distinct band: 28-30N (61.6% of resamples).
N0 most-distinct band: 30-32N (86.6% of resamples).
C3 clustering leaf order: 28-30N -> 30-32N -> 26-28N -> 23-24N -> 24-26N
N0 clustering leaf order: 26-28N -> 23-24N -> 24-26N -> 28-30N -> 30-32N

ECOREGION GROUPING
C3 nearest pair: Costa Central del Golfo + Matorrales Tropicales (28.4% of resamples).
N0 nearest pair: Costa Central del Golfo + Matorrales Tropicales (71.0% of resamples).
C3 most-distinct ecoregion: Desierto de Vizcaíno (38.7% of resamples).
N0 most-distinct ecoregion: Chaparral (26.1% of resamples).

C3 VS N0 DISTANCE-STRUCTURE SIMILARITY
Latitude: Mantel rho=0.430, permutation p=0.2110.
Ecoregion: Mantel rho=0.503, permutation p=0.0023.

INTERPRETATION GUIDE
A high nearest-pair frequency indicates a stable compositional cluster.
A high most-distinct frequency indicates a region repeatedly isolated from
the rest of that trait group's assemblage.
Mantel rho measures whether C3 and N0 share the same overall distance pattern.

STATUS
Exploratory/supplementary. Review biological interpretation before inclusion
in the public retained pipeline.
