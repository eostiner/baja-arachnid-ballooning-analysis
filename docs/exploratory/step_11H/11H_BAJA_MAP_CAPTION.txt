Figure X. Separate geographic organization of ballooning-capable (C3) and non-ballooning (N0) arachnid assemblages across Baja California. Genus composition was analyzed independently for C3 and N0 assemblages using Jaccard dissimilarity after standardizing each latitude band to 22 occupied 25-km cells over 5,000 Monte Carlo resamples. Brackets identify the pair of latitude bands most frequently recovered as compositionally closest, and hatched bands identify the region most frequently recovered as the most compositionally distinct within each trait group. Ballooning assemblages most often grouped 23-24N with 24-26N (56.4% of resamples) and most frequently isolated 28-30N (61.6%), whereas non-ballooning assemblages most often grouped 24-26N with 26-28N (47.1%) and most frequently isolated 30-32N (86.6%). Overall C3 and N0 latitude-band distance structures had Mantel rho = 0.430 and permutation p = 0.2110.
