# Phase 15G v0.4 — Bayesian synthesis figure

## Purpose

This revision uses neutral scientific language and conventional Bayesian effect reporting.
No model is refit.

## Panel structure

- **Panel A:** Frozen C3–N0 Simpson turnover contrasts across the four adjacent latitude boundaries, plus peninsula-wide Simpson and Jaccard summaries. The 26°N point is annotated as a descriptive attenuation toward zero, not a confirmed breakpoint.
- **Panel B:** The 15 observed temporal Simpson replacement contrasts with equal-event resampling intervals, together with the posterior median mean relationship and its 95% credible interval.
- **Panel C:** The standardized stress coefficient shown as a posterior median with nested 50% and 95% credible intervals. The coefficient is reported as `β`, with `Pr(β > 0 | data)`.

## Main-figure exclusions

Posterior-density histograms, held-out CRPS results, prior sensitivity, measurement-error sensitivity, and power simulations remain in the supplementary Phase 15 outputs. They are validation and diagnostic results rather than the primary effect display.

## Interpretive guardrails

- Spatial and temporal analyses answer complementary but distinct questions.
- Negative Panel A values indicate lower turnover in C3 than N0 assemblages.
- Positive Panel B/C values indicate a larger temporal C3–N0 replacement contrast as recent stress increases.
- The temporal analysis remains exploratory because it contains 15 comparisons across 12 cells.
- The Panel B ribbon is a credible interval for the mean relationship, not a predictive interval for new observations.
