PHASE 15 VALIDATION REPORT
==========================
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-07-18

STATIC VALIDATION
-----------------
- All Python files compile successfully with Python compileall.
- The package has no PyMC, Stan, R, or Earth Engine dependency.
- The primary sampler uses NumPy and a transparent Metropolis-within-Gibbs design
  with slice updates for variance parameters.

END-TO-END SYNTHETIC RECOVERY TEST
----------------------------------
A 15-pair, 12-cell synthetic dataset was generated with a true standardized stress
effect of +0.18, cell heterogeneity, Student-t residual variation, and heterogeneous
pair-level observation uncertainty.

Full primary test (4 chains; 2,000 warmup; 3,000 retained draws per chain):
- posterior median stress effect: +0.154837
- 95% credible interval: [+0.052140, +0.256143]
- posterior P(beta > 0): 0.997083
- stress-effect split R-hat: 1.0011
- stress-effect approximate bulk ESS: 737.2

The known positive signal was recovered without a positive prior.

SENSITIVITY VALIDATION
----------------------
- Skeptical, regular, and broad zero-centered priors all retained a positive median.
- Halving and doubling the approximate observation-dispersion scale retained a
  positive median.
- Minimum P(beta > 0) across frozen prior and measurement sensitivities: 0.990667.

PREDICTIVE VALIDATION
---------------------
A reduced-iteration end-to-end leave-one-cell-out test completed for all 12 cells:
- stress model lower CRPS in 10 of 12 held-out cells
- mean CRPS improvement (null minus stress): +0.056845
- exact cell sign-flip p: 0.007324

The full default LOCO settings are intentionally more computationally intensive and
may take several minutes on the user's Mac. The reduced validation run tests program
correctness, not manuscript inference.

POWER-CALIBRATION VALIDATION
----------------------------
With 250 simulations per effect and all 4,096 exact cell sign patterns:
- estimated false-positive rate at beta = 0: 0.040
- first tested effect reaching at least 80% power: +0.20

EDGE-CASE VALIDATION
--------------------
A degenerate synthetic dataset with nearly zero response variance produced poor
variance-component mixing. The Phase 15 diagnostic gate correctly failed rather than
issuing a strong biological conclusion. This is intended behavior.

REAL-DATA LIMITATION
--------------------
The real Phase 14 pair-level files are on the user's local project and were not
available in this runtime. Therefore no real Phase 15 biological result is claimed in
this package. The local run must determine the manuscript conclusion.
