Bayesian estimate of the difference in recent-stress-associated temporal turnover between ballooning-capable and non-ballooning assemblages. Positive values indicate a stronger turnover response among ballooning assemblages. The posterior median was 0.200 with a 95% credible interval of [0.023, 0.359], and P(beta > 0) = 0.989. Results are restricted to repeatedly sampled cells and remain observational.
