## Environmental interpretation from the fresh run

The Spatial+ coefficients were approximately:

- VPD residual: +0.0229, effectively near zero
- Wind-seasonality residual: -0.0790, a modest negative association

The retained interpretation is that broad environmental associations were spatially structured; evidence for an independent VPD effect disappeared after deconfounding, while wind seasonality retained a modest negative association. These are within-sample community-composition associations, not habitat-suitability predictions or causal effects.
